How to Play

Rules

The basics

One player is the Spy (setter): they pick a secret 5-letter word. The other is the Inspector (guesser): they try to guess it, Wordle-style — green means right letter/right spot, yellow means right letter/wrong spot, gray means not in the word.

The twist

After every guess, the Spy may keep their secret or switch to a new word — as long as the new word is still consistent with every clue given so far. A good Spy stalls; a good Inspector asks questions that narrow the field no matter which word the Spy is hiding.

Round 1 is simultaneous

Neither player has any information yet, so the Spy locks in a secret and the Inspector submits an opening guess at the same time, blind.

Scoring

The Spy scores a point for every guess it takes the Inspector to win the round. Fewer guesses is better for the Inspector; more guesses is better for the Spy. Roles swap each round; the lower total score across all rounds wins the match.

Powers

Both sides can unlock special powers that bend the rules in their favor — see the Powers screen for the full list.

Guesser Quests

Every Inspector also gets an always-on Quest: a visible challenge (like using every letter in a keyboard row, or submitting guesses in alphabetical order) that rewards a free green letter reveal when completed. See the Powers screen's Guesser Quests tab for the full list.
