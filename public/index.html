      Reference

      Rules

      The basics

      One player is the Secretkeeper, who starts with a secret five-letter word. The other is the Guesser, who makes Wordle-style guesses. Green means the right letter in the right position, yellow means the right letter in another position, and gray means the letter is not in the current secret.

      The Secretkeeper's decision

      After each guess, the Secretkeeper may keep the current secret or switch to another legal word that still matches every clue already given. Switching is optional. When only one legal secret remains, there is no meaningful choice left.

      Scoring and role swaps

      The opening secret and opening guess are submitted simultaneously. The Secretkeeper scores one point for every guess the Guesser needs to finish the round. Roles swap between rounds. The player whose secret took more guesses to find wins the match; equal scores are a tie.

      Secretkeeper bonus: Stars

      Every legal Keep or New decision earns at least one star. A stronger alternative earns two stars, the best a switch can earn on its own. Changing is optional, but a strong switch can improve the secret and reach reward milestones sooner.

      A bonus-star target may show a letter and position. It comes from a currently best legal secret: one that leaves the Guesser the greatest number of possible words. Matching the target is worth a third star on top of a two-star switch -- the only way to reach three.

      Guesser bonus: Quests

      Quests appear on guesses 2, 4, and 6. Each adds an optional rule to that guess. Completing it immediately opens a choice of three rewards. Later Quest completions have better rarity odds, so the Guesser must weigh the strongest information-gathering guess against fulfilling the Quest.

      Choosing rewards

      Select one of the three offered reward cards. It takes effect immediately unless its description says that it remains active. A reward may improve your position, interfere with the opponent, or change which words remain legal. Each player may use Refresh choices once per game to replace an offer they do not like.

      Swapping sides

      Players swap roles between rounds. Stars belong to the current Secretkeeper's round, while Quests belong to the current Guesser's round. Rewards are earned through those role bonuses rather than through a pre-match power draft.
